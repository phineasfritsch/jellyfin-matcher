How to use the server

Which apps to install on your TV, phone, and laptop, how to request something we don't have yet, and how to settle movie night when nobody can agree.

📺 Watch on your TV

Almost every TV platform has a Jellyfin app. Install it, point it at this server's address, and sign in with the account you were given.

Android TV / Google TV / Chromecast

Official Jellyfin app from the Play Store. Easiest option, best supported.

Amazon Fire TV / Fire Stick

Official Jellyfin app from the Amazon Appstore. Search "Jellyfin" on the device.

Apple TV

Swiftfin (free, official) from the App Store. Infuse works well too if you use it.

Roku

Official Jellyfin channel from the Roku store. Solid, a little plainer than the rest.

Samsung (Tizen) / LG (webOS)

Jellyfin is in both smart-TV stores. If yours is missing, a cheap Fire Stick fixes it.

Kodi

Add the Jellyfin for Kodi plugin if you already run Kodi and want one unified library.

📱 Watch on your phone or tablet

iPhone / iPad

Swiftfin (free, official) is the one to get. Infuse is a slicker paid alternative with better format support.

Android

Official Jellyfin app works fine. Findroid is a great community-built alternative; Streamyfin is another good one.

Each one asks for the server address and your login, then your library and resume points sync everywhere.

💻 Watch on your laptop or desktop

Any web browser

Just open the server in your browser and sign in. Nothing to install. Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari all work.

Jellyfin Media Player

Free desktop app for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Smoother playback and more file types than the browser.

🔎 Want something we don't have?

We use Jellyseerr for requests. If a movie or show isn't in the library, ask for it there and it gets downloaded and added automatically.

  1. 1Open Jellyseerr and sign in with your Jellyfin account (same login).
  2. 2Search for the movie or show you want.
  3. 3Open it and hit Request. For a show you can pick specific seasons.
  4. 4That's it. It shows up in Jellyfin once it finishes downloading.

🍿 Can't agree on a movie? Use Matcher

Jellyfin Matcher is a swipe game for picking something together. Everyone joins on their phone and it guarantees a pick.

  1. 1One person opens Matcher and taps Create Room. A short code and QR appear.
  2. 2Everyone scans the QR or types the code, then picks server-only or any movie.
  3. 3You each check the genres you're up for; a quick knockout narrows it to two.
  4. 4Then swipe: right to like, left to pass, up for maybe, star for a super like. Tap a poster for the trailer and ratings.
  5. 5When everyone likes the same movie it locks in. If the deck runs out first, points decide a winner anyway.
🍿 Open Matcher

Trouble signing in anywhere? Your username and password are the same across Jellyfin, Jellyseerr, and Matcher. If something's broken, ask whoever runs the server.
